Planning is the bridge between a validated initiative and a delivery-ready team plan. After project initiation confirms the problem, success metrics, and stakeholder alignment, the planning phase converts that intent into tractable work. Teams create a prioritized backlog, estimate scope, define a Definition of Done, identify dependencies, and map milestones and release dates. This process keeps planning grounded in evidence and business outcomes rather than broad, undefined requests.

A strong planning process also clarifies ownership and decision-making. Product leaders define the priority and outcome, project managers coordinate sequencing and cross-team alignment, and delivery teams break work into incremental, shippable pieces with clear acceptance criteria. Planning should consider capacity, dependencies, and risk exposure early so that teams can execute without ambiguity. Once the plan is documented and agreed, work can move into execution with a shared definition of success and a clear path to delivery.
